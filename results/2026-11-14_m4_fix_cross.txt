## the crossed and locked episodes need a second measurement before use

The episodes are long: a mean of 18.2 seconds crossed and 17.1 seconds
locked, with a longest lock of 20.1 minutes and a longest cross of 3.5
minutes, at a mean of 430 price units (about 4.3 cents) crossed. Displayed
protected quotations on two exchanges do not lock for twenty minutes under
Rule 610(d), which requires an exchange to avoid displaying a quotation that
locks or crosses a protected quotation of another. The likely reconciliation
is that TotalView-ITCH carries every displayed order including odd lots, and
an odd lot is not a protected quotation, so an odd-lot order may sit inside
another venue's round-lot quote for as long as it rests there.

Correction to the thirteenth-run pre-registration: its limitation paragraph
describes these feeds as "without odd lots, hidden liquidity or the SIP's
processing". That is wrong about odd lots and right about the rest.
TotalView-ITCH includes odd-lot orders; it is the official NBBO that was
computed from round lots. The pre-registration text is left as written, as
every pre-registration here is, and this is its correction.

H54 (fourteenth run) therefore measures the same day again over round-lot
sized levels, and until that is run the numbers above are a statement about
every displayed order, not about protected quotations, and no claim is made
here that any of these episodes was tradable.

Two further limits of this run, both fixed for the next one:
  * The staleness figures below cover the whole file, including the quiet
    hours before and after continuous trading, where a thousand messages can
    span tens of minutes. That is why the mean is 282.8 ms and the longest
    49.5 minutes. From the next run the staleness accounting is restricted
    to the window, as the episode accounting already was.
  * The timed passes covered the first 4,000,000 merged messages, which are
    the quiet open rather than a representative day. From the next run they
    cover the whole file, which is 59,202,007 merged messages.
