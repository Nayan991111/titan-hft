E4: INTERARRIVAL VARIABILITY OF THE REPLAY SCHEDULES. EXPLORATORY CALIBRATION.
Computed 2026-11-20. No thresholds: nothing is being tested. This file exists
because the manuscript quotes the coefficient of variation and the Fano factor
of these schedules and, until now, quoted them from a terminal rather than from
a committed measurement.

WHY BOTH STATISTICS. Kingman's variability term consumes the squared
coefficient of variation of the INTERARRIVAL TIMES. This project had
previously characterised burstiness by the Fano factor of event COUNTS and
drew a conclusion about queueing theory from it. The two are equal for a
Poisson process and diverge sharply for a self-exciting one, which is why they
are now always computed and reported together, on the identical schedule.
